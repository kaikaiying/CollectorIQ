## ASO tips

1. **Update promotional text** regularly (no new build needed) — seasonal, feature highlights.
2. **A/B test subtitle** — try "Drift Test vs Atomic Clock" vs "Track Accuracy • COSC • s/day" after a few weeks.
3. **Screenshots** — lead with value: "Tap to measure" or "See community data" on first screenshot.
4. **Reviews** — ask happy users to rate; respond to negative reviews.
5. **Localization** — add German, French, Japanese for watch-heavy markets later.
